Article I

About the Club

The Small Willy Club is, in the main, a product of chance rather than design. It was never founded so much as it simply occurred — a gathering of individuals drawn together over many years by shared experience, common humour, and a broadly aligned set of values.

No one entirely agrees when it began. The leading theory places its beginnings in the summer of 2003, though the precise origins are happily lost to record.

What started as an accident of circumstance has, in the years since, settled into something more deliberate: a standing fellowship of six, governed by its own bylaws and convened in formal session once a year. The name, we concede, is rather less dignified than the institution it represents.

Article IV

The SWC AGM

Let it be known, and forever recorded: the Annual General Meeting of The Small Willy Club is enshrined within the bylaws of the Club. No power, no authority, no force earthly or otherwise may override it, postpone it, or diminish it. It shall be convened each and every year without fail — and it must be protected, at all costs.

The Current Custodian

As enshrined in the bylaws, the organisation of each AGM falls, in turn, to a single member — the Custodian — who assumes sole responsibility for the weekend that follows. For 2026, that honour belongs to Caleb, who has chosen Nottingham as the destination and now bears full responsibility for its triumph or its ruin.

The Order of Succession

The custodianship began in 2024, conceived as a dignified replacement for stag duties — a supply that had, by then, all but run dry, every member being comfortably married. That supply has, against all expectation, recently shown signs of life: one member, having quietly divorced, now finds himself engaged once more, and a stag duty may yet return to the calendar. The custodianship passes from one member to the next, year upon year, as the bylaws demand:

The Mascot

The SWC Lobster

SWC the Lobster

Official Crustacean of The Small Willy Club

Adopted in June 2026, SWC the Lobster came into the Club's care as part of that year's AGM challenge, set by Caleb: to do something — anything — with £10. The Club's answer was to adopt a lobster, name it in its own honour, and enshrine it forever. The certificate of adoption is displayed above with all appropriate ceremony.

SWC the Lobster remains, at present, in captivity — awaiting release into the open sea off the coast of Cornwall. Upon its release, the date shall be recorded here for posterity, and the membership shall observe a respectful moment of reflection.

The Membership

Club Doctor (PhD, non-medical)

"It's terribly technical. You wouldn't understand."

A cornerstone of the Club. Greg is the only doctor in the SWC — a title he wears with quiet pride and absolutely no medical qualification whatsoever, having earned a PhD in something so overwhelmingly technical that no one is entirely sure what it actually is. He has since moved up north, far enough away that seeing him counts as an expedition — one we gladly make whenever we can, because we love him dearly.

L

Laura "Crusty"

Keeper of Good Cheer

The origin of "Crusty" is officially a mystery, right up until you learn her maiden name was Cristoff — Cristoff, Crusty, and history did the rest. A qualified midwife who has since swapped delivering babies for keeping slightly larger children alive in a school, Laura never strayed far from home, and never needed to. Blessed with a laugh so contagious it ought to be notifiable, she is, by unanimous and unspoken agreement, the best person in any room.

Minister for the Outdoors (and the Van)

Barney exists in a permanent state of 12% battery, tethered to the worst mobile phone known to science and rarely entirely certain which way is up. None of this matters, because Barney is the single finest human being to have a pint with — endlessly lovable, friends with everyone he's ever met, and happiest behind the wheel of his beloved VW van pointed firmly at Cornwall. By day, an outdoor educator teaching children to love the wild. Genuinely — what a man.

Easy-going and outgoing to a fault, Caleb is a carpenter by trade. He loves a bike ride, a surf, and a good gig — and, above all, he is always there. Most importantly, Caleb is the man who first dreamed up the SWC AGM — a contribution of such quiet genius that history will remember him kindly, and so should we.

The Constitution

The Bylaws

As ratified by the Membership and held binding upon all persons herein, in perpetuity.

Bylaw I — Of Membership and Its Limits

1.1The Membership of The Small Willy Club (hereinafter "the Club") shall consist of no more and no fewer than six (6) persons. This number is fixed, sacred, and not subject to the whims, moods, or passing enthusiasms of any individual member.
1.2The number prescribed in 1.1 may be altered only by unanimous agreement of all persons physically present at a duly convened Annual General Meeting (hereinafter "the AGM"). Agreement reached by text message, telephone, hearsay, or whilst intoxicated outside of an AGM shall be deemed null, void, and inadmissible.
1.3No candidate for membership may be considered, discussed, or so much as whispered about unless their name has been formally put forward not less than six (6) months prior to the AGM at which their candidacy is to be decided. This period shall be reserved for the proper and rigorous vetting of the candidate by the relevant members, the standards of which are deliberately left undefined.
1.4For the avoidance of doubt, the Club is full.

Bylaw II — Of the Annual General Meeting

2.1An AGM shall be convened once per calendar year, without exception and without regard to weather, employment, geography, or marriage.
2.2All members shall attend. No excuses shall be entertained, including but not limited to work, illness, distance, offspring, or genuine emergency.
2.3Any member failing to be present for a minimum of one (1) continuous hour of the AGM in a given year may, in principle, be expelled from the Club.
2.4Notwithstanding 2.3, such expulsion shall in practice never occur, by operation of the following mechanism: expulsion requires a unanimous resolution of all six members; Bylaw 1.1 forbids the Membership from falling below six; the expulsion of a member would therefore reduce the Membership to five, breaching 1.1, which would in turn invalidate the very resolution that caused it, thereby reinstating the member, who would then once more become liable to expulsion, and so on, in perpetuity. The Committee has reviewed this loop and resolved, unanimously, to leave it exactly as it is.

Bylaw III — Of the Waffle

3.1Each member shall deliver unto the group a "Waffle": being a video message, transmitted by WhatsApp, concerning the member's life, thoughts, or general goings-on.
3.2A Waffle shall be delivered no less than once per week. Notwithstanding, a member may elect to Waffle once per month, or as and when the mood should take them — provided always that a Waffle is, at some point, and in good faith, actually done.
3.3No Waffle shall exceed five (5) minutes in duration. Any Waffle found to exceed this limit shall be reclassified as a Ramble, which is a separate and lesser form, and shall be held against the member socially.
3.4A Waffle shall be easy to understand. Mumbling, excessive background noise, and the delivery of a Waffle whilst driving are strongly discouraged, though not, at this stage, criminalised.

Bylaw IV — Of the Custodianship of the AGM

4.1The organisation of each AGM shall pass, in turn, to a single member (the "Custodian"), in accordance with the Order of Succession.
4.2The Custodian shall bear sole responsibility for the destination, the itinerary, and the general triumph or catastrophe of the weekend.
4.3The Order of Succession, once fixed, may not be skipped, swapped, or wriggled out of, save by unanimous resolution at an AGM (see Bylaw 1.2, of which the Membership is by now thoroughly weary).

Bylaw V — Of Communications

5.1Each member shall maintain a functioning mobile telephone, charged to a degree sufficient to participate in the affairs of the Club.
5.2Persistent unavailability owing to a flat battery shall be noted in the minutes, sighed about at length, and ultimately forgiven.

Bylaw VI — Of Amendments

6.1These Bylaws may be amended only at an AGM, by unanimous agreement, and in writing.
6.2In the event of any conflict between these Bylaws and common sense, these Bylaws shall prevail.

For the Membership

Frequently Asked Questions

How often must I Waffle?

As per the bylaws, as often as once a week — or as little as once a month, once every two months, once every three months, and so on, in the proud tradition of good intentions. It must, however, be done. Eventually. The Waffle Ledger is watching.

Can I bring a guest to the AGM?

The AGM is strictly for members. Spouses, fiancé(e)s, and children are warmly regarded and firmly excluded. Should a child nonetheless be brought to an AGM, any member present reserves the full and unconditional right to swear at it in an unreasonable manner.

How do I raise a complaint?

Complaints may be submitted in the form of a Waffle, where they will be received, acknowledged in full, and quietly ignored.

I have forgotten my passphrase. What now?

Forgotten passphrases must be confessed to the Committee in person, or at minimum by a Waffle of suitable contrition. The Committee will then perform the Rite of Reset, at its leisure.

Can I leave the Club?

Of course. You are entirely free to go. Simply select "Leave the Club" from the menu, and follow the instructions.
